Questions

Straight answers.

What does "animal-based" actually mean?

Nutrient-dense animal foods — steak, eggs, dairy — plus easy carbs like fruit and honey. No seed oils, no ultra-processed filler. It's not zero-carb and it's not a starvation diet: it's eating what your body was built to run on.

Is it really free?

Yes — the download is free and you get your personalized plan today, no card required. Pro (the coach, Apple Health autopilot, experiments and Weekly Verdicts) has a 14-day free trial, then $14.99/month or $149.99/year through Apple.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No — just an iPhone. Connect Apple Health once and the app reads whatever your phone already collects: sleep, steps, weight and more. A watch adds HRV and resting heart rate depth, but it's optional. Everything is read-only.

Will it fake numbers to keep me motivated?

Never. If you didn't weigh in, that day stays blank — a gap is honest. The Weekly Verdict only reads numbers you actually logged or Apple Health actually measured, and it will tell you when a change was noise rather than progress.

Is this medical advice?

No. Animal Based Coach provides general nutrition and lifestyle information for educational purposes only. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything — always talk to your physician about what's right for you.
